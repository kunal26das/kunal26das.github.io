Which pages this covers

I’m Kunal Das, the author of this portfolio. This notice covers the portfolio homepage, writing index and articles, this page, and the Multidex introduction. It describes the website pages, not the Multidex app or other interactive projects linked from them.

These portfolio pages currently load no advertising or analytics scripts. Other apps and project sites, including projects under the same hostname, can use advertising, analytics, accounts and additional storage under their own privacy information.

Browser preferences

The theme control saves your light or dark preference in your browser’s local storage under the key theme. The portfolio reads it on later visits to keep your chosen appearance. Its scripts do not send that preference to a server or set advertising cookies.

You can remove the preference through your browser’s site-data settings. Clearing data for kunal26das.github.io may also reset data saved by other projects on this hostname. Reading remains available when browser storage is blocked.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts these files. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits made without signing into GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices.

Email contact

Contact links open your email application. If you send a message to kunal26das@gmail.com, I receive your email address and whatever you include so I can respond. Your email provider and mine also process the message.

Use that address for questions about this notice, article corrections, or a request concerning an email you sent me. Include the relevant page or conversation so I can identify it.

Apps and other projects

Yify, DOOM, Startup, Involute and the interactive résumé are separate experiences. This notice does not describe their permissions, cookies, local files or account data. For Yify, including its web app, see the Yify Privacy Policy.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, app stores and other websites take you to services with their own privacy policies. Downloaded apps also have their own privacy information; a description on this portfolio does not replace it.
