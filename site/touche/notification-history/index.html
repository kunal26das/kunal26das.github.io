Save and search notification history on Android

Dismissed an alert you wanted to read again? Touché keeps a searchable history of notifications it captures, so you can return to them later.

By Kudos Labs · Updated 25 October 2026

Start saving new notifications

  1. Install Touché from Google Play and open the app.
  2. Use its notification-access option, read the explanation and continue to Android Settings.
  3. Enable notification access for Touché, then return to the app. New notifications can now appear in history.

Touché cannot recover earlier notifications that are no longer available to it, notifications it never captured or entries already removed from history. Android’s built-in notification history is a separate feature; Touché does not import that history.

Find an alert and choose what happens next

Open notification history and use the search bar to find saved notification text or an app name. Open a result to read its saved details. From there, you can choose to auto-dismiss future notifications from that app or category.

Auto-dismiss runs after delivery. Calls, alarms and protected ongoing activity are excluded from auto-dismiss. It does not stop sound or vibration, block a notification before arrival or revoke the sending app’s permission. For those changes, open the app’s notification settings in Android.

If history looks empty or incomplete

Check that Android still grants Touché notification access, then wait for a new notification. Device and work-profile restrictions can limit capture, and Android may hide sensitive content. Touché cannot reveal content Android does not provide.

Pausing auto-dismiss keeps saving notifications. Turning notification access off stops new captures but leaves saved history readable. If new notifications still do not appear, contact support with your device model and Android version; leave out private notification text.

Understand storage and limits

Free use saves up to 200 notifications. Pro supports up to 5,000 and adds keyword and weekday/time rules, named profiles and CSV export. The free app includes ads; Pro removes them. Separate one-time ad removal does not include the other Pro features.

Saved notification content stays on your device. Optional account sync covers basic preferences, not history. Clearing Touché’s app storage or uninstalling removes its local history; signing in again will not restore it. A CSV export is a separate copy at the destination you choose.
