Find what matters. Choose what stays.

Find a saved notification

Search by app, title or text to find the one you swiped away. History also shows what Touché auto-dismissed and what is still showing.

Clear recurring clutter

Auto-dismiss a whole app or one of its categories, or turn on Dismiss repeats to clear an exact repeat of one you swiped away. Pause Touché to stop saving and auto-dismissing until you resume.

Keep history on your phone

Your history, searches and rules stay on your phone, and there is no account to create. Only purchase records leave it, plus crash reports if you turn them on.

A few things to know

Can I recover notifications from before setup?

Touché saves new notifications after you enable notification access. It cannot recover notifications it never captured or entries that have already been removed from history.

Does auto-dismiss stop the sound?

Auto-dismiss acts after a notification arrives. It does not prevent sound or vibration or turn off the other app’s notification permission. Use that app’s Android notification settings to change those options.

Do I need Accessibility access?

No. Touché uses only notification access, for both history and auto-dismiss. It doesn’t use Accessibility access.

What happens when I pause Touché?

Pause stops Touché saving and auto-dismissing new notifications until you resume it from Settings or the Quick Settings tile. Your saved history stays readable.
