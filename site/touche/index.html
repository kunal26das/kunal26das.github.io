A few things to know

Can I recover notifications from before setup?

Touché saves new notifications after you enable notification access. It cannot recover notifications it never captured or entries that have already been removed from history.

Does auto-dismiss stop the sound?

Auto-dismiss acts after a notification arrives. It does not prevent sound or vibration or turn off the other app’s notification permission. Use that app’s Android notification settings to change those options.

Do I need Accessibility access?

History and auto-dismiss use notification access. Accessibility access is separate and optional: it helps apply selected changes on supported Android settings screens, only after you agree and press Apply.
