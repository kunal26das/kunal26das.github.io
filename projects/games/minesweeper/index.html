Follow the numbers.

Tap a square to reveal it. A number counts the mines in its eight neighboring squares. Mark a suspected mine with a right-click, or turn on Flag mode before tapping.

Use the arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to reveal, and F to place or remove a flag. Flags protect squares from accidental reveals; remove one before opening that square.

Your first reveal opens a safe area. Clear every safe square to win. A mine ends the round, and the whole field opens for review. Some positions need a guess.
