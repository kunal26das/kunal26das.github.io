Leave no square behind.

Join matching letters with horizontal or vertical paths. Paths cannot cross, and every square needs a path.

Drag from a dot, or tap it and then tap neighboring squares. Retrace a path to shorten it; select a dot to redraw it.

Keyboard controls

Tab to the board. Use arrows to choose a dot, then Enter or Space to start. Arrow keys draw the path. Escape stops drawing; Backspace removes the last step.
