Connect Four.

One drop. Two plans.

Build your line while breaking theirs. Connect four across, down, or diagonally before your opponent does.

Disc goes first.

Disc · firstRing · second

Choose a column to start.

Leave room for the next move.

Tap an arrow above the board to drop your piece into that column. Each piece lands on the lowest empty space. A full column cannot take another piece.

On a keyboard, Tab to the column controls, use Left and Right to choose a column, then Enter or Space to drop. The dashed outline marks the last move.

The computer looks several moves ahead. Watch for chances to create two winning lines at once.
